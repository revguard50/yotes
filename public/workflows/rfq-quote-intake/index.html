COMMON QUESTIONS

What manufacturing teams usually want to know.

Does the agent generate the final quote?

The supported first scope is intake and review preparation. A final quote requires customer-specific technical, costing, pricing, and approval behavior. Deeper automation should only be added after those inputs and boundaries are validated.

Can it read drawings and specifications?

The workflow can use multimodal extraction to interpret relevant documents, but accuracy depends on the document set and task. Low-confidence or consequential information should route to a person.

What if every RFQ looks different?

Variation is expected. The agent is configured around the information your team needs, the vocabulary it uses, the documents it receives, and the conditions that should trigger review.

How do we make the ROI model accurate?

Measure how much time is spent on intake and preparation across a representative sample. Validate how much of that work the configured agent can execute reliably, then model capacity and rework from observed data.
